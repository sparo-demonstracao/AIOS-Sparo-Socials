Automated Advertiser
Prospecting & Outreach

Prepared for Susana  ·  August 12, 2026

Your workflow today runs across five tools that don't talk to each other — Monday.com, LinkedIn, RocketReach, Gmelius and Gmail — with a person as the integration layer between them. Every prospect costs the same manual sequence, and the qualification that decides whether a company is even worth contacting happens in someone's head.

This project makes Monday.com the only place you work. You add a company; the system does the rest and comes back to you for the one decision that needs a human — approving the email.

What the system does

01Qualifies— researches each new company and scores it against your ideal advertiser profile
02Finds the right people— BD, Affiliate, Partnerships, UA, Performance Marketing
03Gets their email— through your RocketReach account, verified before use
04Ranks the contacts— by role fit, seniority and data confidence, written back to Monday
05Writes the email— personalised to the company, the contact and the vertical
06Waits for your approval— then sends through your existing Gmail / Gmelius setup
07Runs the follow-ups— and stops the moment someone replies
08Reads the replies— interested, not interested, later, wrong contact — and updates Monday to match

●  The two green steps are the ones that make this more than data entry: nothing enters your queue unqualified, and nothing leaves without you.

How it's built

Monday.com stays your only interface — there is no new tool for your team to learn. Behind it, n8n orchestrates every step and Claude handles the research, qualification, writing and reply classification. Contacts come from RocketReach, and email goes out through the Gmail / Gmelius setup you already use. Everything runs on accounts in your name and stays yours.

One honest note on LinkedIn: it offers no public API for contact discovery, and automating a personal account risks getting it restricted. We source contacts through compliant data providers instead — same result, without putting your account or your GDPR position at risk.
