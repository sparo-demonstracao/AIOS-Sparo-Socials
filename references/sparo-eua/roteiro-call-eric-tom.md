# Roteiro — Call de introdução: Eric & Tom (construtoras NJ)

> **Objetivo único da call:** sair com o discovery pago agendado (1–2 semanas mapeando processos).
> **Papéis:** Enzo abre e fecha; Enzzo aprofunda técnica e cases no meio.
> **Regra de ouro:** não dar preço, prazo nem promessa técnica ao vivo — "that's what the discovery will tell us."
> v1 de 15/07/2026 — refinar depois do alinhamento com o Enzzo.

---

## 1. Abertura (VOCÊ — ~90 segundos, DECORAR)

> "Hey Eric, great to see you again — and Tom, really glad you could join us. Thanks for making the time.
>
> Quick intro for Tom: I'm Enzo, founder of Sparo. We build AI automation systems for businesses — the repetitive office work that eats your team's hours: invoices, follow-ups, paperwork, reports. We make that run on its own, using the tools you already have.
>
> This is Enzzo, my partner on this project. His agency has been building automations for companies for years, so between the two of us you get both the strategy and the hands-on experience.
>
> Before we show you anything, I'd love to understand how things work on your side today — that way we only talk about what's actually useful for you. Mind if I ask a few questions?"

**Logo no início, pedir pra gravar:**
> "By the way — do you mind if I record this call? Just so we can review the details afterwards."

## 2. Discovery (VOCÊ conduz; Enzzo complementa)

Escolher 4–5 conforme a conversa fluir:

1. "Walk me through what happens from the moment a new project is signed — who touches what, in what order?"
2. "What's the most repetitive task your office team complains about?"
3. "Where do things fall through the cracks today — missed follow-ups, late documents, invoice errors?"
4. "How many people are involved in processing a subcontractor invoice, start to finish?"
5. "If you could clone one employee's most boring 10 hours a week, whose would it be?"
6. "What software runs the business today — Procore, QuickBooks, spreadsheets?"
7. "Have you tried any automation or AI tools already? What happened?"

**Enquanto eles falam:** anotar números (horas, pessoas, frequência). São eles que voltam na proposta.

## 3. Momento Enzzo — cases (combinar antes)

Formato de cada case (60–90s): **dor → automação → resultado em horas/dinheiro**.
2 cases no máximo. Nada de tela de ferramenta; história de negócio.

## 4. Respostas prontas — objeções prováveis

**Preço ("how much does something like this cost?")**
> "It depends on which processes we prioritize — that's exactly what the discovery phase answers. The discovery itself is a fixed, small investment, and you walk away with a prioritized plan even if we never work together again."

**Por que não uma agência americana/local**
> "Fair question. Three things: first, you came to us through people you trust. Second, we work your business hours — we're just one hour ahead of New Jersey, so when your team hits a question at 2pm, we're online. Third, this is all we do — AI automation. Not websites, not generic IT."

**Segurança / dados**
> "Everything stays in your systems — we automate on top of the tools you already use. And we're happy to sign an NDA before we look at anything."

**"Will this replace my people?"** (importante pro Tom)
> "No — it takes the repetitive work off their plate so the same team can handle more projects without new hires. Your people move up, not out."

**Prazo**
> "That's what the discovery tells us. But typically the first automation goes live within a few weeks after it."

## 5. Fechamento (VOCÊ — DECORAR)

> "Here's what I'd suggest as a next step: we run a short paid discovery — one to two weeks. We interview a few people on your team, map the processes, and come back with a prioritized plan showing exactly where automation pays for itself first. If it makes sense, we build it together. If not, you keep the plan. Does that work for you?"

E antes de desligar:
> "Great — I'll send you both a follow-up email tomorrow with a summary and the details of the discovery."

## 6. Frases de segurança (quando travar ou não entender)

- "That's a great question — let me walk you through how we'd approach that."
- "Let me make sure I understood correctly — you're saying that…?"
- "Sorry, could you say that last part again? The audio cut out for a second." (salva-vidas legítimo)
- "Enzzo, you've handled this before — want to take this one?" (passar a bola com elegância)
